## Frontier improvements

The initial frontier direction is a configurable interstellar network laboratory
where users select endpoints and intermediate stars, generate traffic, place
hypothetical relays, and compare classical direct, routed classical, and clearly
labeled speculative channels. Acquisition, outages, maintenance, retries,
photon budgets, latency, reliability, queueing, and causality must remain
simulated rather than summarized. Quantum entanglement cannot carry
faster-than-light information; wormhole or spacetime-bending channels must use
explicit hypothetical assumptions and never masquerade as established
infrastructure or demonstrated physics.

## Acceptance gates

- [ ] Users choose endpoints and valid intermediate stars instead of only presets.
- [ ] Packets progress through acquisition, transmission, queueing, failure, retry,
      and arrival.
- [ ] Classical and speculative channels have separate physics and claim receipts.
- [ ] The deep frontier review defines progressive disclosure for complexity.
- [ ] Current desktop and mobile browser reviews prove 3D routes and packet flow.
