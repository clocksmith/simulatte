## Frontier improvements

The initial frontier direction is an interactive evidence investigation rather
than invented traffic motion. Users should build and compare routes, inspect
individual reported incidents and denominators, alter defensible sensitivity
assumptions, and see how evidence changes the ranking. Interest should come from
discovering why two plausible routes differ, identifying evidence gaps, and
testing conclusions, while the experience continues to refuse claims that its
data cannot support.

## Acceptance gates

- [ ] Interaction remains analytical and does not fabricate moving residents,

- [ ] Every route score traces to visible segment evidence and uncertainty.
- [ ] Comparisons distinguish changed evidence from changed modeling policy.
- [ ] The deep frontier review defines a compelling investigation journey.
- [ ] Current desktop and mobile browser reviews prove clarity and engagement.
