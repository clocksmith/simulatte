# Featured interaction observation

Owner: [World product milestone](../../GOALS.md#current-product-milestone). Domain calculations stay in the Motorcycle Noise, Sun Walker, and GPU plugins.

Human observation is pending. No session videos or replay recordings were found in this checkout, no session-replay service integration was identified, and an accessible Pages search for Simulatte user-testing recordings returned no results. This search does not establish that recordings do not exist elsewhere. Browser automation checks pixels, controls, and numerical bindings; it cannot establish first-use understanding.

Use people who have never used these experiences. Open the deployed homepage and say only, "Explore this." Do not name controls, suggest an intervention, or explain the model. Rotate the order of the three experiences across participants. Obtain consent before recording; observations can use anonymous participant IDs.

After exploration, ask: "What is happening? What could you change? What happened when you changed it? Compared with what?" Record their own words, actions, mistaken interpretations, and any assistance supplied. An answer after coaching does not count as unaided understanding.
