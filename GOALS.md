# simulatte goals

## Mission & Thesis

Simulatte helps technical creators turn supported data and instructions into browser simulations they can inspect, edit, and reproduce. World and Blank/Create remain separate products sharing a small browser-native core where contracts genuinely match. Simulation is the product. World illustrates every catalog simulation in the original motorcycle SVG line-art style; illustrations introduce the subject and do not claim to be simulation output. Present every simulation once in a vertically scrolling spotlight catalog with SVG previews, concise descriptions, readiness, and limitations on desktop and mobile. The route catalog owns display names, entry points, readiness, featured status, previews, interactions, and evidence. Qualify Grid, Subsea and Orbital before promoting them; preserve Interstellar’s qualification as it develops. Preserve the hexagon chooser, data tools, and Create implementation.

## Intended Beneficiaries

Creators need to understand the world they requested and make targeted changes without losing prior choices. Engineers need identified inputs, bounded execution, and evidence appropriate to the result. People using supported local data workflows should receive useful execution without first obtaining an account, downloading a model, or depending on a network service.

## Desired Outcomes

1. Complete the input, compilation or mapping, execution, inspection, edit, export, and verified reimport loop.
2. Preserve entities, attributes, relationships, constraints, and negations through the fixed eight-phase prompt compiler.
3. Keep user-authored edits authoritative through the append-only authoring graph.
4. Make simulation outputs and visible behavior inspectable against explicit obligations.
5. Reuse only the runtime and plugin pieces a product actually needs, without making shared code own its interface or lifecycle.
6. Make Motorcycle Noise directly discoverable from the World landing page, with calculated acoustic mitigation, uncertain synthetic sensing, and replayable WorldSpec inputs; allow an explicitly fictional, replayable mist-and-engine-stall interaction; exclude real vehicle interference and unsupported physical or enforcement claims.

7. Open simulations as immediately interactive running worlds. GPU Supercluster models rack computation, communication, and synchronization dependencies, including live straggler interventions; steady-state thermal estimates do not imply thermal evolution. Show GPU connections in a rotatable 3D network layout without altering the modeled topology. Keep a small, deduplicated set of domain measurements visible during execution: acoustic dBA, cumulative sun/shade share, and GPU productive compute share, throughput, and configured steady-state power. Each domain declares measurement subject, time basis, interval, validity, and freshness. Keep current conditions, accumulated history, and predictions distinct. Fictional vehicle stalls are opt-in demonstrations excluded from acoustic baseline comparisons. Object menus retain stable identities while measurements change. Keep configuration in Advanced and observation independent of execution.

8. Establish model accuracy with independent reference cases, timestep or spatial convergence, and separate calibration and evaluation records. Share physical entity identity and units across models and rendering; expose missing coverage and unquantified uncertainty. Improve opening activity and object interactions before adding configuration.

9. Sun Walker first finds the shortest walking path without a shade preference. Its main time-versus-shade slider changes graph search using shadow geometry at predicted arrival times, rather than reranking a few ordinary routes. Show the selected route's extra walking time and reduced sun exposure against that fixed baseline. Keep editable endpoints and top-down/first-person walking views directly accessible.

10. Build Create around reusable interaction contracts: participants, required capabilities, owned state, compatibility, entry and exit relationships, and observable outcomes. Compose and revise those declarations while preserving entity identities and authored choices. Particular activities are acceptance examples, not product branches. Qualified motion providers implement the contracts within their declared coverage; adding a description never establishes a new provider's executability.

## Current product milestone

Pause further catalog expansion while deepening the strongest experiences. Solar
Drive opens as an assembled vehicle operating on one identified NYC route, with
Ride and Inspect views preserving execution and comparison state. Panel shading
connects visible exposure, available solar power, and reserve use against an
identified counterfactual. Reuse frozen street/building inputs and DEM elevation;
evaluate the panel plane at the vehicle's position and timestamp. Published ride
sizes and consumption provide approximate reference context; numerical checks,
matched empirical validation and human observation remain separate qualifications.

Keep the three established featured experiences understandable through interaction while developing the next experiences below. Their unfamiliar-user qualification remains required. Motorcycle highlights the sound paths changed at the observer; Sun Walker displays the accepted route and its actual time/exposure tradeoff against the shortest path; GPU shows how a slow rack holds up dependent racks. Each intervention connects affected objects, a measured change, and an identified comparison baseline. Preserve the numerical qualifications underneath. Observe unfamiliar people exploring without instructions, record whether they identify the activity, discover an action, and explain its consequence, and fix observed barriers. Automated browser checks do not substitute for these observations.

## Authorized bounded World workbench milestone

User direction authorizes System Diagram Simulator as a distinct World catalog
experience at `/system-diagram`, with a declared standalone simulation profile and
node/cluster/link authoring. Its first executable coverage remains the bounded
failure-and-repair experiment; the other catalog priorities remain in effect. Author CPU/GPU task nodes, shared
compute clusters and typed task links; accept a validated architecture revision
explicitly before restarting execution. Reproduce result-store failure after GPU
computation, failed acknowledgement, lease expiry and redelivery. Compare identical
arrivals and fault timing under bounded admission, backpressure, retry budgets and
idempotent result commits. Require exact replay, checkpoint equivalence, reconciled
resources and visible rejected/unfinished requests with causal inspection. This is
a narrow registered simulation milestone, not general catalog expansion or a production claim.

Develop future challenging workloads as composable packs of typed nodes, ports,
resource policies and synthetic/deidentified traces. Streaming/CDN, multi-agent
collection, healthcare, finance, operational data graphs and lakehouse jobs are
representative patterns, not proprietary implementation claims. The staged
[workbench plan](docs/simulatte/distributed-workload-workbench.md) identifies shared
primitives, unsupported semantics and acceptance before additional packs execute.

## Next experience development

Develop Interstellar Relay's delayed-message and return-message interaction, then Asteroid Defense's observation-versus-action experiment. Build Living Tissue first among the three new standalone experiences, followed by River Formation and Crystal Foundry. Each uses the same interaction milestone: active opening, selected-object actions, affected objects, a changed measurement, a named baseline, and reproducible inputs. Keep numerical validation separate from empirical calibration and unfamiliar-user qualification. Retain the existing featured experiences while qualifying additions. Remove superseded paths as consumers migrate; put behavior-changing policy in governed configuration, derive host capabilities from plugin contracts, and reuse bounded numerical and interaction components rather than duplicating controllers or adding a universal simulation engine.

## Operating Loops

For data execution, acquire only the chosen input, validate its mapping and declared semantics, run the selected model, and compare identified results. For prompts, follow the eight phases with each consuming its declared predecessor artifact. Record rejected or unsupported obligations rather than inventing a successful scene. Edit and recompile while preserving accepted overrides, then export and verify reimport against the declared reproduction contract.

## Strategic Constraints

WorldSpec identity, execution identity, renderer behavior, and WorldProof evidence have different roles. Hashes and receipts do not prove physical validity or correct pixels by themselves. Retain the distinct proof classes and evaluate each against its own obligations. Supported determinism must be stated at the relevant level, not as universal identical pixels across hardware. Domain profiles, local data, and prompt models are alternatives with explicit dependencies. Shared core dependencies point inward through documented entrypoints and injected adapters.

## Explicit Exclusions

Do not replace general compilation with fixture recognition, silently narrow user intent, or present an unsupported dynamic claim as rendering success. Simulatte is not unrestricted cinematic generation or a single shell imposed on every product. Doe and Doppler do not define its product scope. [GOVERNANCE_DETAILS.md](GOVERNANCE_DETAILS.md) retains the detailed requirements, phase boundaries, and evidence distinctions.

## Catalog interaction policy

Every simulation autoplays an authored default with audio off. Pause freezes simulation time while inspection and navigation remain available. Restart restores accepted inputs and clears live interventions while preserving camera and running preference. Replay visibly executes recorded actions; Verify replay checks numerical reproduction separately. Completion retains its result and offers Replay. Speed schedules fixed model steps using each domain's declared normal playback mapping; work is retained when rendering falls behind. Selection never moves the camera; Focus does. Supported camera views fit the subject in the usable viewport. Controls communicate immediate display state, live actions, pending scenario edits and accepted values, with keyed identities and rejection rollback.

## World presentation

Every simulation uses the same World UI components. Scenes own composition and
contextual actions; they do not invent control geometry, menus or inspectors.
Supported camera views remain truthful. Field comparison branches share one
viewport through a display switch; neither branch changes the other. Keep Create
separate.

The library at `public/shared/design/world-ui/` combines 256.one restraint with
Simulatte technical drawings: neutral colors, Space Grotesk labels, thin
separators, 4px controls and IBM Plex Mono measurements. Green signals live,
selected, active or consequential state. The library owns interaction states,
menus, inspectors, metrics, sheets and accessibility.

All catalog pages use compact playback and camera docks and one settings sheet.
Mobile has one bottom dock, never a three-row toolbar before the scene. GPU's
scene composition is the default at `/datacenter`; `?frame=scene` remains a
compatible URL. Domain actions and measurement meanings stay with simulations.
The component catalog exposes light/dark and interaction states. CI rejects
simulation-owned native-control styling and shared-component overrides with no
legacy exceptions. Browser checks cover every catalog route; unfamiliar-user
comprehension remains a separate study and is not established by this migration.
