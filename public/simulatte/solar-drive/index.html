Model & evidence +

What is simulated

Fixed-step longitudinal motion, panel irradiance and temperature derating, motor efficiency by speed and load, storage power limits, regeneration, thermal derating, and energy conservation. Initial battery and rotor energy are counted.

NYC street and building snapshots supply the route. USGS DEM samples supply elevation. Sun position follows the declared timestamp; nine samples cover the tilted panel. The stop and weather are scenario inputs. Building surfaces identify the geometry used for direct-beam occlusion; trees, facade details and diffuse sky occlusion are excluded.

Published ride reference

The opening design uses approximate Sun Trip reference sizes: 500 W solar, 1,080 Wh battery and 60 kg unloaded vehicle. The recorded vehicle was a recumbent bicycle with a trailer; this representative assembly, efficiencies, rider load and aerodynamic model remain assumptions. No measured trace was used to tune them.

The optional flywheel remains experimental. Magnetic field lines explain topology; rotor rotation is slowed 120x. No hardware qualification is claimed.

Sources & run identity

    Numerical checks and published ride context are separate. Physical calibration: not performed. Human visual review: pending.
