Model, assumptions & evidence +

What is simulated

Fixed-step longitudinal motion, panel irradiance and temperature derating, motor efficiency by speed and load, storage power limits, regeneration, thermal derating, and energy conservation. Initial battery and rotor energy are counted.

Gravity exchanges potential energy. Magnets exert forces; they add no energy. Solar surplus can recharge reserves. Empty storage and inadequate sunlight can stop the vehicle.

What the image means

The component assembly is representative engineering geometry, not manufacturing CAD. Field lines show topology, not a solved electromagnetic field. Flywheel rotation is slowed 120x to remain inspectable.

Dimensions, material choices, efficiency curves, bearing consumption, and containment mass are assumptions. No rotor structural certification, measured weather, cell aging, or crash analysis is claimed.
